Vaishu

C. Joseph Vijay, also known as Thalapathy Vijay, is an Indian politician and former Tamil cinema actor serving as the ninth Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu since May 2026. Founder and president of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam, he transitioned from a decades-long film career marked by blockbusters like Ghilli, Thuppakki, Mersal, and Leo—many blending social themes with entertainment. Renowned for philanthropy via Vijay Makkal Iyakkam, he cultivated a mass fan movement before officially entering politics in 2024. His party's historic 2026 electoral win broke Tamil Nadu's Dravidian duopoly.
